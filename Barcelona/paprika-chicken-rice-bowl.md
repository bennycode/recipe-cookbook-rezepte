# Paprika Chicken Rice Bowl with Avocado & Yogurt Sauce

**Servings:** 1  
**Prep + cook time:** 25–30 minutes  
**Style:** High-protein Mediterranean-inspired lunch

## Ingredients

- 200 g chicken breast
- 60 g basmati rice (dry)
- 1–2 bell peppers, sliced
- ½ avocado, sliced
- 3 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp lemon juice, plus extra to finish
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. **Make the sauce:** Mix the Greek yogurt with lemon juice, parsley, salt and black pepper. Add a little lemon zest if you like. Set aside in the fridge.
2. **Cook the rice:** Rinse the basmati rice and cook according to the package instructions. The rice shown here calls for 11–14 minutes; follow the packet's water ratio.
3. **Cook the chicken:** Dice the chicken and season with smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Heat 1 tsp olive oil in a pan and cook until golden and the thickest pieces reach **74°C / 165°F** internally.
4. **Sauté the peppers:** Add the remaining 1 tsp olive oil to the pan. Cook the sliced peppers for 6–8 minutes, until softened but still slightly crisp.
5. **Assemble:** Serve the rice with the chicken and peppers. Add the sliced avocado and spoon the yogurt sauce over the top or serve it on the side. Finish with fresh lemon juice and black pepper.

## Chef's Extra Tips

- Add the avocado in slices on the side for a cool, creamy contrast.
- Add finely grated lemon zest to the yogurt sauce for extra freshness.
- Finish the chicken with freshly ground black pepper and a squeeze of lemon.
- For a neat presentation, arrange the rice, chicken, peppers and avocado in separate sections of the bowl.
- If peppers bother your digestion, cook them until soft and start with a moderate portion.

## Approximate Nutrition

- **Energy:** 590 kcal
- **Protein:** 50–55 g
- **Carbohydrates:** about 65 g
- **Fat:** about 15 g

*Nutrition is an estimate and varies with ingredient brands, avocado size and oil quantity.*
