# Protein Breakfast Bowl with Berries, Banana, Almonds & Chia

A quick, creamy breakfast bowl with protein, fruit, fibre and healthy fats.

**Servings:** 1  
**Prep time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook time:** None

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain queso fresco batido 0% or plain Greek yogurt
- 75 g fresh blueberries
- ½ banana, sliced
- 15–20 g almonds
- 10 g chia seeds
- Optional: 30–50 g rolled oats for extra carbohydrates and a more filling breakfast

## Method

1. Spoon the queso fresco batido or Greek yogurt into a bowl.
2. Add the blueberries and sliced banana.
3. Scatter the almonds and chia seeds over the top.
4. If using oats, stir them into the yogurt or sprinkle them on top.
5. Serve immediately.

## Chef's Extra Tips

- Choose plain, unsweetened yogurt to avoid unnecessary added sugar.
- Use queso fresco batido 0% for a lighter, protein-rich base; check the label because protein content varies by product.
- For easier digestion, start with a modest amount of chia seeds and chew them well.
- If you train in the morning or need more energy, add oats or another piece of fruit.
- If you prefer a softer texture, mix the chia seeds into the yogurt and let the bowl stand for 5–10 minutes before adding the toppings.

## Approximate Nutrition

For 200 g queso fresco batido 0%, 75 g blueberries, ½ medium banana, 15 g almonds and 10 g chia seeds:

- **Energy:** about 380–430 kcal
- **Protein:** about 22–25 g
- **Carbohydrates:** about 35–45 g
- **Fat:** about 15–18 g

Values are estimates and depend on the yogurt brand, fruit size and topping quantities.
